#### 1. 🏍️ Motorcycle Helmet Detection: YOLOv12 vs RT-DETR
A deep learning project that compares modern object detectors for automatically detecting helmet usage on motorcycle riders, to support traffic safety monitoring.

- **Models:** YOLOv12 and RT-DETR as main models, with Faster R-CNN, YOLOv8, and YOLOv11 as baselines.
- **Approach:** Followed the CRISP-DM framework, from dataset exploration (class balance, image quality, duplicate detection) to preprocessing and model training.
- **Dataset:** Custom-collected helmet / no-helmet images, including tricky cases like hoodies, beanies, and bicycle helmets.

#### 2. 🧠 MentalUX: Online Psychology Consultation Platform
A mental health consultation website that connects users with verified psychologists. Built as a team of 5 across the **E-Business** and **User Experience** courses, covering business analysis, UX research, system design, and user validation.

**Problem:** Indonesia has roughly **1 clinical psychologist per 91,900 people** (WHO ideal: 1:30,000), heavily concentrated in Java. MentalUX addresses this access gap and supports **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being**.

- **Key Features:** Psychologist listing with transparent pricing, consultation scheduling & categories, QRIS / bank transfer payment, real-time chat room, educational articles & videos, satisfaction survey, and **MentalUX AI** (chatbot for early support).
- **Role-Based Access:** Separate flows for **Customer**, **Psychologist** (certificate upload, schedule & transactions), and **Admin** (psychologist verification, transactions, article management), with a Figma prototype of 27+ screens.
- **UX Research:** Design Thinking (Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test) using the **5/5/5 concept** (5 journals, 5 similar websites, 5 interviewees), plus surveys, observation, personas, wireframes, and iterative revisions based on user feedback (login/reset-password flow, booking flow, UI consistency, rating feature).
- **Business & Strategy:** Market potential, competitor benchmarking (6 platforms), customer segmentation, SWOT, USP/EVP, investment proposition, revenue & cost projection, and a TikTok marketing campaign plan.
- **System Design:** Waterfall SDLC with use case & activity diagrams, business flow chart, and a security plan (risk assessment, WAF, security audit).
- **My Contribution:** Problem identification & objectives, system architecture, business flow chart, value proposition metrics, EVP summary, investment proposition, and SDLC methodology.

#### 3. 🏍️ CRT APP: Online Sales Mobile App for an Adventure Motorcycle Dealer
A mobile app design for **CRT (Cilangkap Racing Team)**, a dealer selling KTM, Husqvarna, and Sherco motocross & adventure bikes. Built as a team of 4 for the **IS Analysis and Design** and **Database System** collaboration project.

**Problem:** CRT's sales were offline-only, which limited market reach to local customers. Manual record-keeping caused stock and pricing errors, and the sales process was not integrated.

- **Key Features:** Product catalog (motors, spareparts, accessories), search by category, wishlist, cart, checkout, QRIS / bank transfer payment, product reviews & ratings, and stock management for admins.
- **System Analysis & Design:** **Use Case, Activity** (browsing, ordering, payment, stock management), and **Class Diagrams** (customer, order, payment, product, admin), plus functional and non-functional requirements.
- **UI/UX:** Interactive **Figma** prototype of the full buying flow, from sign up and login to checkout, payment, and order confirmation.
- **Methodology:** **Prototyping**, with user feedback driving each revision.
- **User Testing :** 94.3% said the app meets mobile e-commerce standards, 97.1% found it relevant, 88.6% approved the color scheme, and the average rating was **4.47 / 5** .
- **Iteration:** Revised based on feedback by implementing **Dark Mode** and a cart quantity adjuster.

#### 4. 🫁 COVID-19 Chest X-ray Classification: XGBoost vs CNN
A data modeling project that classifies chest X-ray images as **Normal** or **COVID-19**, comparing a traditional machine learning approach against deep learning, and deploying the final model as a web app. Built as a team of 4.

- **Models:** **XGBoost** (baseline, on flattened pixel features) vs **Convolutional Neural Network** (main model with Convolutional, MaxPooling, and Batch Normalization layers).
- **Data Preparation:** Used the COVID-19 Radiography Database from Kaggle, handled class imbalance with **undersampling**, and applied **RGB image augmentation** that preserves the lung anatomy.
- **Deployment:** Wrapped the trained CNN in a **Streamlit** web app where users can upload an X-ray image and get a prediction in seconds.
- **My Contribution:** Data exploration (EDA) and report writing.

#### 6. 🎓 Data Warehouse for Scopus Publication Analytics
A data warehouse and Power BI analytics project that consolidates lecturer master data and Scopus export files at **Universitas Multimedia Nusantara** (2020-2025) into a single source of truth for monitoring research performance. Built as a team for the **IS545** course.

- **ETL & Data Quality:** Built an end-to-end ETL pipeline in **Power Query**, covering data cleansing (name standardization, lecturer and publication deduplication) and data conforming (standardized publication types, venues, and CiteScore quartiles).
- **Dimensional Modeling:** Designed a **star schema** with fact tables (FactPublication, FactPublicationAuthor) and conformed dimensions (lecturer, study program, faculty, venue, publication type, date), including surrogate keys and Slowly Changing Dimensions.
- **Dashboards:** Built a **Power BI** semantic model and interactive reports for quartile performance by faculty, publication trends per study program, and cross-program collaboration.
